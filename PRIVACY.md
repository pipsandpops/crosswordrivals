# Crossword Rivals: Word Battle — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 15, 2026_

Crossword Rivals: Word Battle ("the game") is made by **Bhawana Pareek**, an individual developer in India.
Contact: [pipsandpops02@gmail.com](mailto:pipsandpops02@gmail.com)

## What the game itself stores
Your progress (level, coins, hints, ranks, Daily Puzzle streak, wins and settings) is saved only on your device. The developer runs no server and does not collect accounts, names, emails, contacts, photos, files or your location. Uninstalling the game deletes this progress.

## Daily reminder
If you turn on the Daily Puzzle reminder, the game schedules a local notification on your device each evening. No data is sent anywhere.

## Ratings
The game may show Google Play's own rating card. Your rating goes to Google Play, not to the developer.

## Adverts (Google AdMob)
The game can show optional rewarded videos (for a free hint or double coins), a small banner advert at the bottom of the screen, and an occasional full-screen advert between matches. Adverts are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure adverts, Google may collect and process device identifiers such as the advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, information about your interaction with adverts, and diagnostic information. See [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for consent using Google's User Messaging Platform, and you can change your choice in Settings ("Privacy options") when available. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

## Purchases (Google Play Billing)
Remove Ads and hint packs are handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details.

## Your choices and data deletion
Because the developer stores no personal data, there is nothing for the developer to delete. Your game progress lives only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game. For data processed by Google for adverts, use the "Privacy options" in the game's Settings (where shown), your device's advertising ID settings, or the Google Privacy Policy linked above. You can always email the developer with any privacy question.

## Children
The game is a general-audience word game for teenagers and adults and is not directed at children under 13.
